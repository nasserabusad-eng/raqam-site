How to learn division fast?

Division isn't a brand-new operation — it's a multiplication question flipped around. When you see "45÷5?", ask yourself "5 times what equals 45?" and you land on 9 right away. That's why mastering the times tables is the fastest route to mastering division.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the division table?

The division table is the mirror image of the times table: if 3×4=12, then 12÷3=4 and 12÷4=3. It helps you work out division results quickly.

How do I learn division easily?

Master the times tables first, since every division is really a multiplication question in reverse: "12÷3?" means "3 times what equals 12?" The answer is 4.

What is 56 ÷ 7?

56 ÷ 7 = 8, because 7×8=56.
