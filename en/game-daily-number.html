How to play Daily Number

Every day there's a new secret 4-digit number with no repeated digits. Type your guess and press Check: a green tile means a correct digit in the correct spot, yellow means the digit is in the number but in the wrong spot, and gray means it isn't in the number at all. You have 6 tries to find it — and everyone gets the same puzzle each day, so you can compare results with friends.
