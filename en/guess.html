How to play Guess the Number?

The computer picked a secret number between 1 and 100. Type your guess and press "Guess", and you'll get a hint: is the secret number higher or lower than your guess. Use the hints to narrow the range until you land on the right number. The smart trick is to always guess the middle of the remaining range (binary search) — that gets you to any number in 7 tries or fewer!
