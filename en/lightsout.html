How to play Lights Out?

The board is made of lit and unlit tiles. Tapping any tile flips its state along with its four neighbors (up, down, left, right). The goal is to switch off every light. It looks simple but it's a deep logic puzzle: each tile needs to be pressed either once or not at all, and the order you press them in doesn't matter. Start with the 3×3 grid, then challenge yourself with 5×5.
