How to play Make 24

You're given 4 random numbers. Pick two numbers and an operation between them (add, subtract, multiply, or divide) and they merge into a single result that takes their place. Repeat until only one number is left — if it's exactly 24, you've won the round! Every round here is guaranteed solvable, so if you get stuck, try a different order of operations. The game trains fast arithmetic thinking and forward planning.
