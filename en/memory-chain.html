How to play Memory Chain?

Numbers light up on the keypad one at a time, forming a sequence. Watch carefully, then tap the same numbers back in the order they appeared. Get it right and the sequence grows by one more digit next round. One mistake ends the game, and you'll see the longest chain you reached. A classic game that builds short-term memory and focus.
