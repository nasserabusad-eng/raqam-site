How to play Number Memory

All cards start face down. Click two cards to flip them: if the numbers match, they stay face up; if not, they flip back after a moment. Try to remember where each number is to match pairs in as few moves as possible. Pick the difficulty that suits you — more pairs means a bigger challenge for your memory and focus.
