How to solve number sequence puzzles

Look at the difference between each number and the next: is it constant (an additive pattern)? Is each number double the last (a multiplicative pattern)? Or are the numbers perfect squares (1, 4, 9, 16)? Once you spot the rule, predicting the next number becomes easy. These puzzles show up often in IQ tests and job interviews, and practicing them builds logical and mathematical thinking.
