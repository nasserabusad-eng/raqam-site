How to play Number Snake

Guide the snake toward the glowing number on the board — you must eat the numbers in order: 1, then 2, then 3, and so on. Each number you eat lengthens the snake and shows the next target somewhere random on the board. Watch out: hitting the wall or the snake's own body ends the round immediately. How many numbers can you collect before you slip up?
