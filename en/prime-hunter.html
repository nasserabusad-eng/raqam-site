What are prime numbers?

A prime number is a natural number greater than 1 that's only divisible by itself and 1 (like 2, 3, 5, 7, 11...). Tap every prime number you spot on the grid before time runs out — tapping a correct prime earns a point, and tapping a non-prime counts as a mistake. Once you've cleared the whole grid, a fresh one appears automatically. Pick a difficulty based on the number range you want to practice.
