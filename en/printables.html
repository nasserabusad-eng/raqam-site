Why printed worksheets?

Writing by hand cements the times tables in a child's memory far more than reading alone. Print a different table's sheet each day, and let your child fill in the blanks, then check their answers against the table at the top of the page. Once they've got it down, move on to the interactive practice on screen.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I print the times table worksheets?

Open the worksheet for the table you want and print it from your browser (or press Ctrl+P on a computer), then choose your printer or "Save as PDF" to save it to your device.

Are the worksheets free?

Yes, every worksheet is free to use at home and in the classroom.
