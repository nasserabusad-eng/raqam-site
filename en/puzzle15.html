How to solve the 15 Puzzle?

The board has 15 numbered tiles and one blank space. You can move any tile next to the blank into its spot. The goal is to arrange the numbers in ascending order from 1 to 15 in the fewest moves. The trick is to solve the first row, then the second, and leave the last two tiles of each row for the end. A classic game that builds planning and spatial thinking.
