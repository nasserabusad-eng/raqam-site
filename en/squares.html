Square Numbers & Square Roots

The square of a number is that number multiplied by itself (like 5²=25), and the square root is the reverse operation (√25=5). Here's a table of square numbers from 1 to 20 with their roots.

How to memorize square numbers?

Start with the small squares up to 12², since they come up most often. Notice the patterns: squares of numbers ending in 5 always end in 25 (15²=225, 25²=625), and the difference between consecutive squares is an increasing odd number (1, 4, 9, 16... with differences of 3, 5, 7...).

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the square of a number?

The square of a number is that number multiplied by itself. For example, the square of 6 is 6×6=36, written 6².

What is a square root?

A square root is the reverse operation of squaring: the square root of 36 is 6, because 6×6=36. It's written with the symbol √.

What is 12 squared?

12 squared is 12×12=144.
