How to play Sudoku

The goal is to fill a 9×9 grid with the numbers 1 through 9, so that each number appears exactly once in every row, every column, and every 3×3 box. Tap an empty cell, then choose a number from the keypad below. Cells shown in blue are given and can't be changed. Start on Easy and work your way up to Hard.
