How to play Sum to Ten?

The grid is full of numbers from 1 to 9. Tap two numbers that add up to exactly 10 (like 4 and 6, or 2 and 8) and they'll vanish and get replaced by two fresh numbers, earning you a point. Pick two numbers that don't add up to 10 and both tiles flash red and get deselected. Rack up as many points as you can before time runs out (90 seconds). A fast game that sharpens your speed at finding complements to 10.
