How to memorize the times tables fast?

Start with the easy tables (2, 5, and 10), then gradually move to 3, 4, and 6. Say each table out loud, then test yourself in "Practice" mode until you can answer without thinking. Remember that multiplication is commutative: 7×8 = 8×7, so memorizing half the table is often enough.

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the easiest way to teach kids the times tables?

Start with the easy tables (2, 5, and 10), then move to 3 and 4, and use patterns — for example, the digits of the products in the 9 times table (from 9×1 to 9×10) always add up to 9. Short daily practice with an interactive quiz works better than one long memorization session.

Is there a full printable times table PDF?

Yes, the Full Times Table page is ready to print or save as a PDF, and we have worksheets for every table from 2 to 12.

At what age should a child start learning the times tables?

Usually in 2nd and 3rd grade, after they've mastered addition and understand multiplication as repeated addition.
