Number Tricks 🎩

Simple tricks that let you calculate in your head faster than a calculator

Ad space

Mental math isn't a talent — it's a set of small tricks that pros know. Try the tricks below, then test your speed in the Mental Math game.

1 · Multiplying by 11 in a flash

To multiply any two-digit number by 11, add the two digits and put the result between them.

63 × 11 → 6 (6+3) 3 → 693

If the sum of the digits goes over 9, carry one to the left: 78 × 11 → 7 (15) 8 → 858.

2 · Squaring any number ending in 5

Take the tens digit, multiply it by the digit after it, then stick 25 on the end.

35² → 3 × 4 = 12 → stick on 25 → 1225

85² → 8 × 9 = 72 → 7225. Works for any number ending in 5, however big.

3 · The 9 times table on your fingers

Spread both hands out in front of you. To multiply 9 × n, fold down finger number "n" from the left. The fingers before the folded one = the tens, and after it = the ones.

9 × 4 → fold down the 4th finger → 3 fingers | 6 fingers → 36

4 · Flip the percentage

"x% of y" always equals "y% of x". Flip the problem to make it easier.

4% of 75 is tricky? Flip it: 75% of 4 = 3

To find 10%, just divide by 10, and to find 5%, take half of the 10%.

5 · Multiplying by 5, fast

Multiplying by 5 = dividing by 2, then multiplying by 10 (or the other way around). Much easier than multiplying directly.

46 × 5 → half of 46 = 23 → add a zero → 230

6 · Multiplying by 4 and by 8 with doubling

Multiplying by 4 = double the number twice. Multiplying by 8 = double it three times.

17 × 4 → 34 → 68 · 17 × 8 → 34 → 68 → 136

7 · The divisibility-by-3 test

Add up the digits of the number; if that sum is divisible by 3, so is the whole number.

2571 → 2+5+7+1 = 15 → 15 is divisible by 3 → so is 2571 ✅

8 · Subtracting from 1000 the easy way

Subtract each digit from 9, and the last digit from 10.

1000 − 476 → (9−4)(9−7)(10−6) → 524

Why do number tricks work?

These tricks aren't magic — they're shortcuts built on the properties of numbers. The more you practice them, the more automatic they become, so you can work out your bills, discounts, and totals in your head without paper. Start with one trick a day, and drill it in raqam's games until it sticks.
